Level the field

Production company: MusaFilms
Role: Production Assistant & Creative Support
Scope: Social Media Campaign & Educational Content
Year: 2023

Teaching teenagers about discrimination usually fails the moment it feels like an institutional lecture. The problem is simple: kids tune out moralizing rhetoric, making traditional educational programs completely ineffective on social media.

Our solution was to frame social equality through the lens of street football logic. Instead of preaching, the campaign uses legendary Dutch football philosophy to show that respect, inclusion, and standing up against prejudice are just basic field tactics. Hosted by street football icon Rocky Hehakaija, the film drives youth and educators directly to the Fairplay educational platform, where classrooms engage with an interactive online game, positioning exercises, and open debates.

Rules on the pitch, rules in life.
