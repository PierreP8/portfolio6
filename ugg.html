Love shot providers

Agency: Yard Agency
Role: Creative Concept, Art Direction & Casting
Scope: Social Media Content, Casting & Video Series
Client: UGG
Year: 2023

The Feels Like UGG campaign bridges UGG with European street culture on a permanent loop. We bypassed standard product placement to create genuine cultural resonance, using love as an editorial filter. Instead of traditional faces, we cast local “Love Shot Providers”: rising talent representing diversity, radical self-belief, community, and pure authentic style.

The Cast: Four European voices shaping their own scenes: Danny Lomas (London), Lucky Love (Paris), Simran (London), and Matyouz (Paris). Minimalist studio setup, unfiltered answers.
