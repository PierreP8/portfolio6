Drink life to the fullest.

Agency: New Business
Role: Art Direction & Creative Concept
Scope: 360° Campaign
Client: Source Alma / Thonon
Year: 2025

Reinventing Thonon required shifting a heritage Alpine water brand into a dynamic, intergenerational everyday choice. We created the tagline “Boire la vie en grand” (Drink life to the fullest) to reconnect pure hydration with warmth, family, and modern lifestyle.

The hero TV spot playful twists formal sommelier rituals, featuring a young boy serving Thonon to his family during an outside lunch in the Alps.
