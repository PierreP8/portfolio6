Sticky business

Agency: Self-Initiated
Role: Art Direction, Copywriting & Production
Scope: Super Bowl Activation, Product Innovation & Spec Campaign
Year: 2026

Super Bowl food culture glorifies the mess, but no one actually wants glaze under their fingernails while trying to act normal. We solved the issue directly on the shelf by strapping a custom wipe pack onto a limited-edition packaging.

Instead of chasing noisy, loud Super Bowl slapstick, the film turns a simple product tweak into a study of social survival. We played on the uncomfortable tension between primal food indulgence and modern etiquette. By pairing tight, uncomfortable close-ups of sticky fingers with a slow jazz soundtrack, the contrast makes the simple act of wiping your hands feel like the ultimate power move. The product stops being a sauce bottle and becomes an essential game-day flex.

Getting messy is optional.
